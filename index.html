Buying guides

UIDBuy spreadsheet shoes and clothing

Keep a shortlist with the seller link, exact size and color, and the measurements you used to make your choice.

  • Measure both feet and use the longer foot length when comparing sneaker sizes. Ask whether the chart gives foot length or insole length.
  • Compare a hoodie’s chest width, sleeve length and body length with a hoodie you already wear. For a jacket, measure over the layer you plan to wear underneath.
  • For pants and shorts, check waist, rise and inseam. For matching sets, confirm the top and bottom sizes individually.
  • Check T-shirt fabric weight and jersey chest width. For headwear, compare head circumference with the cap’s adjustment range.

UIDBuy product links

If you find an item on Weidian, Taobao or 1688, save the original seller URL and the item ID. Reopen the live product page before checkout and select the exact color and size you saved.

Check the stock status for that variant, any minimum order quantity, domestic delivery fees and the seller’s return window. If the listing changed, ask the seller to confirm the model and variant before paying.

UIDBuy QC photos

Compare warehouse quality-control photos with the variant you selected. For shoes, check the outsole, toe shape and both size labels. For clothing, request a flat measurement photo with the tape visible from end to end.

For jewelry, ask for a clasp close-up and chain length. For a hat, compare the brim shape and closure. If there is a mismatch, check the exchange deadline before approving shipment.

UIDBuy shipping estimate

Ask for packed parcel dimensions and actual weight. Check whether the shipping route bills by actual or volumetric weight. A rigid shoe box can increase the billable weight, so compare the packed dimensions with and without it if both options are offered.

For an international order, check tracking, insurance limits and applicable import charges for your destination. Include the item subtotal, domestic delivery and service fees when comparing the total cost.

Frequently asked questions

What is a UIDBuy spreadsheet?

It is a browsing list that groups product links by category. Open the live listing to confirm the exact item, variant and current availability before ordering.

Are the products pictured here available through the category links?

The brand photos illustrate product styles. The category links open HipoBuyFind, where you can check current listings. This guide is independent of UIDBuy and the pictured brands.

How do I choose a sneaker or clothing size?

Measure your foot or a garment that fits in centimeters and compare it with the seller’s chart for the exact variant. Confirm whether the chart uses body measurements or garment measurements.

Do QC photos prove an item is authentic?

QC photos show visible details such as color, stitching, measurements and condition. For authenticity, check the seller’s authorization and look for verifiable proof of purchase.

How can I estimate international shipping costs?

Check the carrier’s rates for your destination and ask which weight it uses for billing. Add domestic delivery, service fees and any applicable import charges to estimate the total cost.
